Bench configuration

Single-bank flash (nDBANK = 1). HWVAL fills sectors 0–10 and keeps sector 11 free for its destructive flash tests. It is flashed over SWD.

Product configuration

Dual-bank flash (nDBANK = 0). The bootloader is in bank 1 and there is one 976 KB application slot per bank. The GUI switches between the two configurations over SWD.

03 · A/B bootloader

Updates that can't brick the board.

An update only ever writes the inactive bank. A new image boots on trial and must confirm itself; otherwise the bootloader returns to the last good image.

BANK 10x0800 0000
BOOT32 KB
META A16 KB
SLOT A · application976 KB · 0x0800 C000
BANK 20x0810 0000
reserved32 KB
META B16 KB
SLOT B · application976 KB · runs at 0x0800 C000 via SWP_FB

The bank swap bit SYSCFG_MEMRMP.SWP_FB maps slot B to the same address, so one binary runs from either slot. The bootloader never erases its own sectors, and no option byte is written to switch slots.

1
UploadSent in 1 KB chunks. Only the inactive slot is erased and written.
2
VerifyThe header and image CRC32 are checked against what was read back from flash.
3
ActivateThe slot gets a higher sequence number and is marked pending, then the board resets.
4
Trial bootThe bootloader starts it with the IWDG running and counts the attempt.
5
Confirm or roll backA healthy app confirms. After 3 failed boots the old image boots again.

04 · HWLINK protocol

One schema for every device.

The GUI and all three firmware images share one FlatBuffers schema. Frames are COBS-encoded and end with 0x00; the CRC32 check lets the receiver resynchronise after any error.

05 · Hardware validation

Every block. Tested, or explained.

Tests that need missing equipment report SKIP instead of failing. Destructive tests are confined to flash sector 11 and need explicit confirmation.
